MapleScanner design

Tooltip blueOSMS cream

One idea: it should feel like a piece of the game's own interface that happens to know prices. Dark tooltip blue, the game's own bar colours for meaning, and a rail that answers "is this good?" before you read a number.

True size beside a real capture. Shop and listings are read from that capture. Market prices and channel are made up for this mockup.

Colours

Measured from the capture, then lifted to stay readable on the dark panel. Colour is never the only signal: every verdict also has words.

Verdicts

Only a deal or a warning gets a filled tag, so a stall full of "fair" looks calm and a deal stands out at a glance. The tall tick is what it sold for, the dot is this listing, and a dotted rail means thin data. No rail at all means we don't know yet. NPC prices below are real, market prices are made up.

Fonts

Prices come first. Nunito keeps 0, 3, 6 and 8 apart at small sizes, its digits are all the same width so prices line up, and its rounded ends suit the game. Fredoka adds character, for headings and verdicts only. Both are open licence and get bundled, because the app works offline.

Stall card states

All true size. The card shrinks when there's nothing to show, so it isn't sitting beside the game as an empty box.

Popups

The player's eyes and mouse are on the game, so the rule is: nothing may take focus from the game, and nothing may ask for a click while they're playing.

Needed while playing
Stall cardThe small always-on-top window. Shows the open shop, and shrinks to one line between shops. Never takes keyboard focus. Replaces today's plain pop-out with its Windows title bar.
Item cardSits inside the stall card, not a new window. Appears while an item is hovered in the game and stays until the next one, so the player can move their mouse away and still read it.
Problem stripOne slot inside the stall card, for frozen picture, no channel yet, database offline. Stays until fixed, says what to do, needs no click.
Needed only outside play
Game pickerOnly when the game window can't be found by itself. Already exists, gets restyled.
Delete confirmationReplaces the Windows confirm box in the price database. Says what's lost, and the red button names the action.
Result line"Imported 12 items and 340 listings." A line in the page that fades away. Already exists as text, gets a fixed place.
Deliberately left out
Windows notifications, soundsA shop is only read once the player opens it, so there's nothing to alert them about. Alerts belong with the watch list, which is deferred.
Deal popupDeals already sort to the top of the card with the loudest colour. A second signal would only cover the game.
"List it at" promptRuled out: a single number from the tool would become the market's price.
Setup wizard, tipsSetup is one button on the home page. The problem strip handles anything that goes wrong later.

Main window

Scanner and prices become two tabs in one window, instead of a page that opens another page. Most of today's explanation text moves out of the way, and the OSMS credit stays on this page, as it has to.
