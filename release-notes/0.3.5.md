## Dark theme

MapleScanner now has a dark theme. Pick it under **Theme** in Settings:

- **Match Windows** (the default) follows Windows: dark when Windows is in dark mode, light otherwise.
- **Light** or **Dark** keeps the one you choose.

The stall card and the Prices tab follow it too, and switching fades over a moment instead of flashing. If your Windows is in dark mode, MapleScanner opens dark after this update. Choose Light in Settings to keep the cream look.

## Softer cream

The light theme's background is a deeper cream, easier on the eyes than the near-white it was.

## Remembers its window

MapleScanner now opens where you left it, at the same size, and maximized if it was. If that screen isn't connected any more, it opens in the middle of your main screen.

## Tidier pages

- Scanner, Settings and About now line up with the Prices list in the middle of a wide window. On the Scanner tab the stall card sits right next to the buttons.
- The confidence dots in the Prices list are all one colour now. How many are filled still shows how sure the price is.
- An empty box next to Updates on the About tab is gone.
