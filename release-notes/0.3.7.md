## Real bargains can show as deals

An item listed far below its usual price, like a 100K item at 10K, used to stay on **check** for good. It still starts there, because a price that low is often a misread. Hover the item in the shop: once MapleScanner has read its name from the tooltip, the row turns into a deal with how far under it is.

It only does this when enough sellers agree on the usual price and nobody else asks less.

## Usual prices closer to what things sell for

What's left for sale is often what nobody wanted at that price, so a usual price taken from asks ran high. When three or more sellers have sold an item for less, the usual price now follows those sales. Warrior Potion went from 2,000 to 349.

A price that looks far too low next to the asks, but matches what the item sold for, no longer gets a **check** warning.

## When an NPC pays more

Some items sell on the Free Market for less than an NPC pays for them. The Prices tab now shows the NPC's price next to such a usual price.

In your own shop, an item that would pay you less than an NPC after the 3% fee is marked **NPC**, with what each would pay you.

## Hovering fixes a name that wasn't read

When a row says its name wasn't read, hovering it now corrects the name in more cases, and it stays corrected. A name that couldn't be read shows as **Unread item** instead of a garbled name. Items with a slash in their name, like Silver / Black Stealer Pants, are read properly.

## Prices on the card stay up to date

The stall card now refreshes its prices every 10 seconds. Before, it only did so after reading someone's shop, so your own shop could show **no data** for every item right after starting the app.

Nothing to do: everything applies as soon as you update.
