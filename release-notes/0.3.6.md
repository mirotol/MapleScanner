## No more false deals while hovering

With the game's cursor resting on a price, MapleScanner could read only part of it: a 19,999 item showed as 199, or even 1, and was called a deal. A price with the cursor on its digits is now left alone until the cursor moves, so the card keeps the price it read before.

A price that's missing a digit for any other reason is left out too, instead of showing as a lower one.

## Your own shop stays private

While you set up your own shop, the stall card still shows each item with how its price compares, so you can see whether you're listing cheap or dear before you open. It now says **Your shop, not shared**, and nothing from that screen is sent to the shared prices. Before, items in a shop that wasn't open yet could be sent as if they were for sale.

Nothing to do: both apply as soon as you update.

## Clearing a search

The search box on the Prices tab has its own × button to clear it.
