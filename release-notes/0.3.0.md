## Choose whether to share your scans

MapleScanner shares the shop listings it reads so every player's prices stay fresh. Sharing is now your choice:

- The installer has a new **Sharing prices** page that shows what is sent, with a box to turn it off.
- **Share my scans** in Settings turns it on or off at any time, without a restart.
- With it off, nothing is sent and you still see everyone else's prices. The anonymous install key is only made the first time a scan is shared.

If you're updating, sharing stays on. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Firmer prices

A price now counts as firm (●●●) only once 5 different sellers have sold the item, up from 3, on at least 2 different days. Until then the usual price comes from the cheapest asks, as before. This makes it harder to fake a price with a few made-up sales.

## Installer

The installer now shows a few pages instead of installing in one click, so it can ask about sharing. It still installs just for you and needs no administrator rights.
